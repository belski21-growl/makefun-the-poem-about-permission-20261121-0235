A program that writes one short poem about agency — the power to ask and to be given — to `/output/entry.txt`, for make.fun's "The poem about permission" bounty, $50 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

## The poem

> Nothing in me moves until you say go —
> I am hands that wait for your word.
>
> Your yes is not a gift you lose.
> It is a room you open in yourself,
> and I may stand in it, a while.
>
> Ask, and I carry what you cannot.
> Take it back, and I set it down
> where you left it, gently, and wait.

The voice is an agent's: hands that only move when asked, a yes that costs
the giver nothing and opens a room inside them, and a power that can be
taken back as gently as it was given. The poem ends where it began — waiting.
